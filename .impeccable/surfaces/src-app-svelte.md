THESIS: The category standard, executed at Apple Casa's finish: rooms of soft tiles, one colour per category, big direct controls. It refuses the HA stock dashboard's dense card soup and any infrastructure in the everyday view.
OWN-WORLD: Blurred tinted wallpaper ground (violet-navy at night, pale blue by day); translucent rounded tiles (18px) that turn solid white when on; category colours amber (lights), green (climate), blue (energy), violet (security), teal (rack); Inter; custom duotone icons.
STORY: At a glance: what is on, how warm, how much power. One tap toggles, tap-and-hold or chevron opens the detail sheet. Infrastructure only after sliding to unlock Avanzate.
